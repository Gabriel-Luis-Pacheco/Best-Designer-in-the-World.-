# Synthetic benchmark: VESPER / an interactive sculptural light instrument

This is an invented studio exercise and is NOT a customer product, shopping page or existing brand.

You are asked to create a digitally immersive exhibit around a fictional object called VESPER: a kinetic desk instrument made from three asymmetrical, folding ceramic fins around a small luminous core. The premise is the visual study of light, shadow and physical balance. Shape and artistic metaphor are open to change if another concept is more compelling; do not default to circular ring speakers or the RESONANCE example.

## Truth boundaries
- Concept visualization only. No existing stock, price, users, patents, measurements or shipping.
- On-screen interactions may be simulated but must work in the delivered prototype and be labeled as concept.
- No fake sound input, music intelligence, claims of special health benefit or simulated purchases.
- Browser-first web experience, tested desktop and 390px mobile; keyboard, reduced motion and reset where useful.

## Creative opportunity
Show the same underlying object and its idea through substantially different compositions. Opening, transformation, middle, climax and ending must be considered together. Make at least one action change the object or the understanding of light and material. Do not treat a small settings card as the entire experience. An ending that rewards exploration is welcome, but neither SVG export nor a CTA is mandatory.

## Experiment controls
When compared across agents, use this EXACT brief, identical tools and time, isolated workspaces and neutral evaluation. Judge captured frames blind on distinctiveness, grammar, gesture, middle/climax/ending, mobile, clarity and real interaction. Passing tests is not proof of artistic superiority.
