# CEO × CMO × sales × growth × customer journey — task-driven commercial studio

Load when a **public/commercial** site or product workflow needs materially better acquisition, conversion, activation, retention, or revenue. For a pure art site, operational dashboard, one CSS fix, or non-commercial document, omit this guide unless those business goals were explicitly requested. The Design OS is the owner of the whole experience; specialist skills are optional collaborators, not instructions to create committees.

## 1. Name the actual bottleneck before assigning specialists

Classify **OBSERVED**, **USER-REPORTED**, **HYPOTHESIS**, **UNKNOWN** and **BLOCKED** data. Do not infer "low conversion" from an unattractive screen alone. Use actual behavior, customer language, alternatives, channel/search intent and evidence. Select one primary metric linked to the user's job and the business, plus safeguards. A page may underperform because of traffic quality, offer, positioning, pricing, trust, onboarding or value—not just visual design.

| Customer step | Decision/question | Primary owner or specialist lane | Real evidence and a useful artifact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Market/problem discovery | Who needs this and why change now? | CEO/CPO + customer research + competitor profiling | Interviews only if real, buyer alternatives, actual unmet job, dated research |
| Awareness/distribution | How do qualified people find us? | CMO + content/social/ads + SEO/GEO/AEO | Search/page evidence, channel intent, traffic source, verified listings |
| Landing/interest | Can someone understand the difference quickly? | Art + UX + positioning + Great Leads/copy | Honest promise, product demonstration, message-match, first-fold comprehension |
| Consideration/trust | Why believe this works and is right for me? | CRO + sales enablement + ethical psychology | Actual product behavior, truthful terms, relevant proof, objections and risk |
| Signup/checkout | Can I act without friction or surprises? | UX + CRO + pricing + signup + accessibility | Successful task, errors, price clarity, consent, real payment path |
| Activation/first value | Does the product deliver the promise promptly? | Product UX + onboarding + growth | Time to real value, completion, activation event meaning |
| Retention/customer value | Why return voluntarily? | Product + growth loops + support/customer success | Cohort behavior, repeat useful sessions, feedback, accessibility |
| Referral/expansion | Who would recommend or buy more—and why? | Growth + community + ethical referrals | Verified satisfied users, voluntary recommendation, eligibility |
| Sales/B2B | Can a buyer justify the decision? | Sales + offer + objection handling | Demo, documented case, buyer process, pipeline stages and CRM hygiene |

A good sales funnel is **not** a compulsory page structure. Remove sections that answer no new user question. Respect an established user-selected identity. Do not invent social proof, fake urgency, client names, price discounts, sales wins or trial statistics.

## 2. Team rules (CEO / COO / CMO / sales / growth / psychology)

**CEO / strategic owner:** handles real product-market priority, budget constraints, tradeoffs and cross-functional decisions, with founder/legal authority limits. The user's own https://github.com/Gabriel-Luis-Pacheco/skill-ceo specializes in the **BataTalk** organization. Only invoke its specific operator role when the work is authorized and actually about BataTalk; otherwise use general business strategy and do not import BataTalk policies into an unrelated client's website.

**COO / execution operations:** maps owner, deliverable, source truth, dependency, acceptance condition and observable result. It is a role to assign when real agent delegation exists, NOT evidence that a specific COO skill is installed. Use proportional handoffs; no fixed agent count.

**CMO / go-to-market:** chooses market, channels, message, creative brief, offer, measurement and cost. Use Marketing Skills marketing-plan or product-marketing if actually available. Do not force a marketing strategy on a task that only asks for color adjustment.

**Sales:** understands buyer doubts, eligibility, price transparency, demos, objections, outreach-to-response quality, handoff to CRM. If prospecting is requested, research and draft legitimate outreach; actual external sending or data access requires permission and applicable platform policy. Do not mass-message, impersonate, spam or collect sensitive data without authority.

**Growth:** distinguishes acquisition, activation, retention, referral, revenue and actual sustainable value. Work from a measurable, low-cost experiment, clear denominator and guardrail; do not demand viral features or dark patterns.

**UX + behavioral/consumer psychology:** focus on attention, expectations, trust, cognitive effort, progress signals, perceived risk, consent and decisions. This is a **behavioral design research lens**, not a clinical diagnosis or a claim that an AI agent is a licensed psychologist. See ethical-user-psychology.md.

**Design & engineering:** preserve brand identity and task flow, propose one strong visual concept, implement full states, verify real UI and accessibility. Do not let marketing specialists override platform behavior, rights or real product facts.

## 3. Route the right external expertise instead of reciting every skill

Use the Design OS specialist orchestrator first: inspect available native skills, then conditionally consult exact source SKILL.md of the relevant external specialty. Registry file: research/specialist_registry.json. Read its scope guards (notably owner's BataTalk CEO skill).

Example selection:
- No buyers, unclear message and weak landing: customer research + product marketing + Great Leads/copy + art lead + CRO; then actual user acquisition experiments.
- Conversion broken on checkout: signup/checkout CRO + accessibility + QA; **not** a full rebrand or content factory.
- Organic discovery absent: SEO audit + AI SEO/GEO if appropriate + site architecture/content strategy; check actual indexability, keyword intent and verified crawl behavior.
- Social/ads campaigns: channel-specialist + actual offer proof + analytics and funnel handoff; don't publish content or spend without permission.
- Retention poor: product task observation + onboarding + growth analytics; no extra pricing CTA until first value is proved.
- Enterprise/B2B: positioning, sales enablement, prospecting (consent), actual demo proof, buyer objection handling.
- Expressive design without marketing: one art/motion skill plus QA, **not** CEO, CRO, SEO and sales.

## 4. Evidence-first funnel measurement

Define events and their **meaning**, not just a number: qualified visit, demo-view (actually viewed), CTA click, signup-start, account-created, first useful task completed, activated, paid checkout confirmed, retained cohort, referral. Keep denominator, timestamp, source, platform, attribution, deduplication, consent and broken/partial journeys explicit. A CTA click is not a customer; a signup is not a retained user.

Track funnel stage conversion as a *matched cohort*: stage completions/stage-qualified entrants under a declared time window. Avoid comparing incompatible acquisition windows. Data missing = UNKNOWN, never zero. Paid and unpaid channels require different cost/trust checks. Separate business result from user benefit and monitor harmful incentives.

Use one hypothesis-to-experiment card:
- The observed problem and evidence source, with confidence.
- Which user action should improve, without a made-up uplift.
- The smallest falsifiable change to offer/creative/UX/channel.
- Test setup, guardrail, sample/reviewer plan, and stop/rollback.
- Actual before/after measured outcome (or NOT_RUN), owner and next decision.

## 5. Great Leads application to commercial design

Load great-leads-ethical-copy-framework.md for the right opening mechanism and buyer awareness.
