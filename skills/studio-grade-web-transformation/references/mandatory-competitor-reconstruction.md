# Reference-first competitive reconstruction — MANDATORY full-funnel mode

**Trigger (MUST RUN):** build a new landing page, substantially redesign a landing page, or create/rebuild a major system journey (onboarding, sign-up, pricing/checkout, CRM pipeline, dashboard, settings, activation, etc.) where comparable products exist. Small PATCH, emergency fixes and narrow adjustments to an approved composition are exempt: reuse previous research or inspect only the relevant comparable scene. A user saying “use the skill”, “like Salesforce”, “human-designed” or “copy what the leaders do” activates this protocol for the relevant scope. This is a production **prerequisite**, not another optional inspiration article.

## The governing decision

**Do not invent from zero when a comparable, inspectable, mature pattern exists.** Start from what leading competitors **actually ship**, and transfer the *functional architecture* with high fidelity: the questions answered, message order, persuasion roles, layout hierarchy, information density, CTA priority, step sequence, progress feedback and interaction logic. Choose one **dominant reference** for each major target scene/step, with secondary references only where they solve a concrete gap.

**Fidelity target:** “recognizably the same proven category convention/decision sequence” — **not** “recognizably another company’s unique visual identity.” Reuse familiar structures and generic interaction patterns; produce your own brand expression, accurate product media, original copy, icons, motion assets and code. Do not lift proprietary words, imagery, video, mascots, distinct creative compositions, source code, restricted assets or confusing trade dress. Do not imitate deceptive upsells, dark patterns or inaccessible controls.

**Evidence honesty:** being used by Salesforce, HubSpot or another successful company is **OBSERVED_ADOPTION**, not evidence that any individual headline, motion, onboarding screen or pricing section was A/B-tested, caused revenue growth or is appropriate for our users. Prefer same-job patterns supported by multiple real implementations or independent UX research. Mark a hypothesis until we test it. If our own trustworthy user evidence contradicts the benchmark, favor that evidence and document why.

## 0 — Set the reference set BEFORE writing JSX or copy

1. Identify our real user, market, product capability, account/pricing/legal constraints, first useful outcome and target environment. Preserve the current product’s working contracts and verified brand assets. A website marketing request does not authorize unrelated backend/account/billing changes.
2. Research **6–10 relevant direct competitors/substitutes** when feasible for a flagship LP and **2–4 high-craft cross-category exemplars** for specialist visual treatments. For a full product system, deeply inspect **3–5 comparable competitors** and add targeted component examples; do not substitute a gallery of homepage thumbnails for working journeys. If fewer are accessible, record which were attempted, why, and what external research can actually support the missing mechanism. Do **not** fabricate coverage to meet a count.
3. Choose 1–2 **primary benchmark products** best matched to the audience, business model, product sophistication and user job — not just the biggest logos. A Salesforce-like CRM may compare Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, Freshsales, Attio, or Close, but each is a **candidate** until the live page/flow is inspected.
4. Save exact URLs, observation date, viewport, locale, route, state, source of each screenshot, and access limitations. Record what was PUBLIC, actually explored with authorized test credentials, or NOT_OBSERVED. No bypassing paywalls, access controls, private accounts, rate limits or purchasing without user authorization.

## 1 — **Scroll every important competitor page to the end**

For each **primary** competitor, capture a real **full-page desktop screenshot and full-page mobile screenshot**; then inspect and document **every visible section, in order**, not just hero and pricing. Expand accordions, tabs and menus; examine sticky behavior and navigation if accessible. Record all important branches and CTAs. When screenshots cannot be produced, identify the limitation and mark **NOT_VERIFIED**; a tool’s text-only scrape is not a visual review.

Build an observed section ledger. Each source section gets:
- **Order + role:** navigation, opening hook, category recognition, pain, product demonstration, use cases, proof, differentiation, objections, integrations, pricing, final CTA, legal footer, etc. Never force this list as a template; record **what is really there**, including absences.
- **Visible writing strategy:** headline *function* (promise, risk, category, specificity), actual meaning in your own words, word count, sentence shape, objection addressed, subhead relationship, action verb, CTA destination, proof proximity, audience language and emotional register. Study **every meaningful heading, subhead, CTA, caption, card, form field, disclosure and error**, not only the first slogan. Store analysis/paraphrase; do not republish copyrighted competitor copy.
- **Art direction and geometry:** dominant focal object, visual reading path, content/media ratio, columns, vertical spacing, font roles/scale, real asset crop, illustration/photo/3D treatment, section height, background transition, density, rhythm and mobile rearrangement.
- **Media and motion:** actual photos, authentic product screenshots, illustration, video, animation, how motion triggers, what changes, length/easing when observed, controls, fallback, loading and reduced-motion behavior. Video frames and interaction states need separate evidence; **never infer timing or behavior from a screenshot**.
- **Behavior and strategy:** primary vs secondary CTA, navigation, sticky elements, demo entry, objection sequencing, pricing disclosure, lead capture, trust claims and evidence limits. Record source proof claims as **company claims** unless independently substantiated.

A **primary competitor is not deeply inspected** if all the agent has is its hero, marketing copy scraped without a visual capture, a single full-page thumbnail, or a list of claimed "best practices".

## 2 — Reverse engineer the entire funnel and real product, when in scope

For each accessible comparable journey trace:

`traffic/source -> landing -> CTA -> signup/demo -> qualification/permissions -> welcome -> onboarding choices -> first key task -> first meaningful result -> return path`.

Record **each screen and branch**: URL/state, form labels, order of questions, number of decisions, optional/required, defaults, progressive disclosure, progress, skip/back, validation, empty/loading/error/success, confirmation, email state when lawfully accessible, desktop/mobile, time-to-value hypothesis, and clear proof of the path actually observed. Include pricing / plan and cancellation terms where relevant. A video/demo is *not* proof you completed a live onboarding.

For **system UI** rather than marketing, inventory only the requested modules (e.g., CRM contacts, leads, pipeline, tasks, activities, permissions, reports, search, import). For EACH requested module compare the comparable category **list -> filter/search -> empty -> creation -> detail -> edit -> success/error -> return**. Note information architecture, controls, interactions, hierarchy, shortcuts, density, data definitions, and role-specific states. Avoid copying competitor features that our product does not offer. Mark unviewable private/paid screens clearly.

**The target scope must be covered.** A new homepage does not require reverse engineering every Salesforce admin screen; a request for a whole CRM cannot stop after making a landing page.

## 3 — Build a benchmark reconstruction blueprint

**Before implementation**, create an annotated map of the target output:
- **Whole-page storyboard:** target section IDs in actual intended scroll order. For each one, the visitor decision, selected dominant competitor + source section ID, source screenshot, its headline/CTA/proof role, semantic/visual composition, media and motion, and what to **ADOPT / ADAPT / REJECT**. Reuse the observed successful-in-market category pattern as default; do not swap it for an original card-grid template without a concrete reason.
- **Copy map:** customer awareness stage, hook structure, claim specificity, objection, proof and CTA for every target section. Write **new text with the same strategic job and comparable brevity**, supported by our actual product; don't translate or paraphrase another company's full copyrighted copy. Reject generic model phrases even when they satisfy keywords.
- **Interaction map:** exact source flow step(s) -> our matching step(s); preserve familiar input/feedback architecture wherever appropriate. Explain each unavoidable divergence (different product capability, audience, brand, accessibility, privacy, legal terms).
- **System-wide visual grammar:** typography roles, spacing rhythm, content widths, density, surface hierarchy, photography direction, media proportions, navigation and mobile breakpoints. A coherent own-brand grammar should connect borrowed mechanisms; the result should not be a collage of competitor logos/styles.
- **Explicit invariants:** brand assets/identity, truth of features, prices, rights, auth, permissions, data, accessibility, consent, performance budgets and functional routes.

**No ungrounded design freedom:** when a good match exists, the agent must implement it before proposing an alternative. Deviation is earned by a documented risk, incompatible scope, observed weakness or own user evidence, **not** “more creative”, “more premium”, “AI design taste”, personal preference or random gradients.

## 4 — Reconstruct at high fidelity in real code

1. Keep the chosen reference open or in a dated screenshot board while implementing. Code the equivalent **section architecture, proportions, emphasis, text length class, media scale, decision order and interaction behavior**, substituting our product’s original, truthful content and licensed assets. Do not transfer recognizable protected creative expression.
2. Reuse well-supported **UI mechanics** on onboarding, forms, navigation, trials and dashboards (including empty/error/disabled/return states) instead of inventing unusual controls. Preserve privacy/accessibility and terms even if a competitor obscures them.
3. Build the ENTIRE requested deliverable — not just a new hero followed by unchanged weak sections, and not only a gorgeous landing page with a broken CTA/onboarding.
4. Capture **reference vs before vs implementation** at matched desktop and mobile viewports, plus meaningful motion states and each applicable onboarding stage. Audit side by side:
   - Same visitor question and proof? Same deliberate information order and scan path?
   - Comparable **visual weight**, typography craft, crop, negative space, CTA prominence and section rhythm?
   - Does the copy perform the same persuasion role without copying text or inventing claims?
   - Does the click really reach the next step and meaningful outcome?
   - Do mobile hierarchy and important states correspond?
5. **Fail the deliverable** if any important target section lacks a mapped observed reference or a documented justified exception, if the agent never inspected source pages below the hero, if critical funnels are claimed without step evidence, if sections are still generic template filler, if real media is replaced by fake UI, or if important P0/P1 visual/functional defects remain.
6. Repair **the actual source scene or flow**, recapture and test again; do not fix a structurally weak section with glows, shadows, random motion or another section. An independent reviewer when available should see **the brief, competitor source, target screenshot and functional state**; blind human preference testing is separate and optional until actually performed.

## 5 — Mandatory evidence packet, not a self-awarded certificate

For substantial marketing pages or system rebuilds, fill `templates/COMPETITIVE_RECONSTRUCTION_PACKET.example.json` and run:

```bash
python scripts/competitive_reconstruction_gate.py competitive-reconstruction.json --root .
```

The packet requires: actual primary benchmark URLs/captures, a **real full-scroll section ledger** and copy/motion observations, every requested target section mapped to a source section, all in-scope onboarding/system steps mapped, implemented source paths, actual destination paths/screenshots and reviewer findings. A JSON shape with fake claims is not enough; the validator checks **coverage, evidence-file existence, image dimensions and content consistency** but cannot prove a browser visited the site, detect copyrighted trade dress, judge taste, or verify conversion. Mark unavailable access honestly. If the gate reports `BLOCKED`, `INVALID` or `NEEDS_EVIDENCE`, do not announce “reference-validated premium LP”; explain precisely what remains.

### Salesforce-style illustrative briefing (NOT claimed as a completed live audit)

- Compare the **live** landing hierarchy and audience segmentation of Salesforce and HubSpot; Pipedrive/others for direct product demonstration and trial entry. Source screenshots and individual sections must be observed and indexed *in this run*.
- Transfer the **job and order** of opening category/value claim -> proof and actual workflow -> role/industry objections -> integrations/trust -> price/lead action, only when those steps were observed and are relevant. Never infer identical sections without inspection.
- If the ask includes CRM onboarding, compare the real signup/setup/lead-first-value pathways that are accessible. If the ask is for the full system, investigate the requested CRM modules and their states, not merely marketing copy.
- Do not label Salesforce/HubSpot/Pipedrive choices “experimentally validated” without published, applicable experimental proof. Their public adoption is useful prior evidence.
