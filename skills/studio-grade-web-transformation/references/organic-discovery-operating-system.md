# Organic Discovery OS — category leadership, not metadata theater

**Activate:** every public site where acquisition/findability matters, including LANDING, MARKETING, COMMERCE, public documentation, service portfolios and content hubs; respect explicit AUDIT/PATCH scope and user choice not to be indexed. **Objective:** maximize *qualified organic demand captured across appropriate surfaces* through technical eligibility, original usefulness, authority, trust and an exceptional post-click journey. No agent can guarantee outranking every competitor or an arbitrary organic growth curve.

**Related guides:** `seo-discovery-and-search-experience.md` (page/technical), `ai-answer-and-entity-visibility.md` (AI citations), `search-content-and-authority-engine.md` (content + distribution), `search-intelligence-measurement-loop.md` (measurement). Read only the needed branches, not all for a tiny change.

## 0. Model the business and SERP landscape before choosing a page template

A site has to be *discovered for a real question* before its landing design can matter. The design agent owns discovery strategy where within authorized scope; it must not make the user enumerate SEO acronyms. Build a compact internal picture using **actual** data/tools, or label inputs `HYPOTHESIS`:

- Actual company/product, viable customer profile, value events, location/languages and business model; what can be genuinely proven and demonstrated?
- Discovery demand: branded vs unbranded, problem-aware vs solution/comparison, learn vs choose vs act, conversational queries, visual/product/local intents and follow-up questions.
- Real organic alternatives: competing websites and **SERP competitors**, directories/aggregators, forums, social/video, ecommerce listings and AI-generated summaries; these differ from direct business competitors.
- Topical opportunity: which underserved user jobs can we answer **uniquely**, better than a generic summary and with defensible primary evidence?
- Existing discoverability: indexed and canonical URLs, queries/impressions/clicks, lead or signup quality, crawl issues, citations and authoritative linking/mentions, as actually observed.
- Constraints: supported language/region, right to publish, expertise/reviewers, technical stack, brand/legal promises, realistic production cadence.

**Intent-to-page match:** one *distinct* useful page/feature per meaningful user task. Group synonymous queries on a single canonical page when they answer the same need; split only if intent, relevant product proof or decision context truly differs. Never create near-duplicate city, keyword, competitor or translation pages purely to multiply coverage.

## 1. Multi-surface discovery model (each has its own evidence)

| Surface / jargon | Practical work | Measurement / caution |
|---|---|---|
| Organic web SEO | Crawl, understand, index, earn relevance and selection in Google/Bing | Search Console/Bing impressions, clicks, queries, pages; ranking depends on context |
| GEO / AEO / generative search | Make useful answers easy to verify, attribute and cite in AI search | Google generative AI impression report; Bing AI Performance citations; not guaranteed |
| LLMO / AI visibility | Maintain accurate official brand/entity references, trustworthy evidence and a retrievable site | The name is not a universal protocol or separate magic ranking algorithm |
| Search experience optimization (SXO) | Strong page-message match, useful first action, conversion and satisfaction | Qualified business outcomes, not raw impressions alone |
| Local search/Maps | Authentic local entity, verified profile, service area, business details, location-specific proof | Only if a real eligible local business; no fake offices |
| Ecommerce/shopping | Real product/variant data, feed/merchant eligibility, pricing, availability, returns and accurate Product markup | No fabricated aggregate ratings or stock |
| Image/video/multimodal search | Owned meaningful media, contextual pages, alt, captions/transcripts, video discovery where eligible | Rich surfaces may or may not show |
| International / multilingual | Actual localization, correct hreflang/locale URLs, native terminology, regional trust and offer | Translation alone is not market fit; avoid blanket geo-clones |
| Discover/editorial | Original timely or evergreen reporting, genuine expertise, relevant media and eligibility | Highly variable distribution; never promise impressions |
| Marketplace / social / community discovery | Useful contributions where audience actively searches; ethically earned mentions | Off-site distribution supports awareness, not a substitute for site quality |

Not every site should use every channel. A purely online SaaS is *not* automatically a Google Business Profile candidate. A private dashboard should not be indexed. An artistic portfolio may win organic interest via work/case studies, image/video search and relevant citations, not 100 articles.

## 2. The leadership workflow — discovery → advantage → release → learning

**A. Establish scope.** For PATCH fix explicit SEO defects without inventing a content department. For a new commercial site or major redesign, organic visibility is an integrated workstream (while protecting privacy/rights and scope).

**B. Inspect actual search surfaces.** If authorized browsing and search data exist, sample relevant country/language/device-intent SERPs. Record date, location, query, real result URLs, what each result satisfies, who dominates, gaps, and whether local results, shopping, video, snippets, AI answer or community material appear. Do not claim "top 10 competitor audit" if you did not actually inspect them.

**C. Audit the site's source and deployed reality.** Check crawlable routes and content, HTTP status/redirects, robots, noindex, canonical, sitemap, navigation/orphans, JS rendering, hreflang (if multilingual), metadata, media, structured data, accessibility and mobile. Verify deployed URL and browser states where tools exist. `scripts/search_presence_audit.py` is static and cannot prove indexing; Google Search Console is the authoritative property evidence.

**D. Select an earned edge.** Win because of *substance*: differentiated product demonstration, primary data, verified comparisons, case work, original explanatory visuals/video, interactive tool/calculator, field research, credible author review, documentation, real helpful community answers or reliably updated original records. Match the proof format to the actual searcher's task. Never default to "publish 50 posts."

**E. Design URL/topic architecture.** Prioritize a small set of high-fit pages/experiences: homepage (brand/category), product or service page (decision), individual use cases (distinct tasks), comparisons when fair and substantively different, original research/guides/tools and necessary trust/contact/legal pages. Maintain coherent internal links to help people and crawlers follow meaningful relationships; no keyword-first doorway/parasite networks.

**F. Ship truthfully.** Produce page-specific titles, headings, answer/proof sections and visual interaction that genuinely match query intent, plus accurate preview/JSON-LD and working conversion path. Preserve creative art direction: crawlable content is not permission for generic keyword blocks or hidden text.

**G. Establish legitimacy.** Explain who made the thing, why it exists, real experience, limitations and how to verify. When relevant, cultivate legitimate mentions, expert co-creation, partnerships and citations. No purchased link schemes, fake reviews or synthetic authority.

**H. Measure → diagnose → repair.** Search visibility → eligible clicks/referrals → engagement/qualified action → activation/revenue/satisfaction, separately by audience/intent/market/device/page. Diagnose *where* the bottleneck is: missing indexing, weak query fit, low recognition, trust/proof, UX or post-click failure. Iterate from evidence, not hypothetical CTR multipliers. Also watch technical/SEO regressions after any redesign.

## 3. Content and design must strengthen each other

Do **not** sacrifice the authored experience to SEO. Integrate real content *inside the correct visual medium*: searchable transcripts for authentic videos, meaningful project descriptions for portfolios, SSR/server HTML around interactive scenes, accessible diagrams of real processes, original product demos, accurate breadcrumbs/site navigation and page-specific visual proofs. A compelling image without indexable surrounding meaning can be invisible to some crawlers; walls of optimized text can destroy the very site visitors were promised.

Apply a post-search **message-match chain**: query intent → actual title/snippet/AI answer proposition → landing visible answer/proof → qualified CTA → functioning first value. An unrelated hero creates an expensive leak even with high ranking.

## 4. Separate growth metrics from unsupported fantasies

Possible strategic outcomes: nonbrand qualified organic visits, activated users from organic search, valuable inquiry rate, branded search demand, share of visibility in a defined market/query sample, important landing page impressions, Bing AI citations, Google generative AI impressions, revenue per eligible session. Define the **exact cohort/market/query basket, measurement method and period**. No invented search volume, keyword difficulty, SEO score, share of voice, citations, traffic or competitor outcomes. A self-assembled sample of prompts is directional, not market share.

Prioritize by **observed impact**, visitor value, evidence quality, implementation effort, risk and reversibility; avoid fabricated weighted scores. Technical blockers and false content beat tiny metadata optimizations. A legitimate competitor outranking you is a research signal, not authorization for spam or deceptive tactics.

## 5. Surface-specific routing (avoid one-size-fits-all)

- New SaaS: use cases and practical demo/guide tied to a functioning product; compare honest alternatives; ensure docs/pricing and activation.
- Enterprise B2B: decision-stage technical docs, integrations, real case methodology, security attestations only if verifiable, appropriate demo/inquiry path.
- Local services: legitimate service area, location evidence, eligible business listings, trust and real contact; do not invent cities or an address.
- Ecommerce: product/collection URLs for distinct inventory and needs, feed/merchant data, variants, stock/shipping, visual discovery and actual checkout.
- Education: authentic learning task, demo, curriculum/objectives/limitations and evidence of pedagogy; no fabricated learning efficacy.
- Creators/portfolios: distinguishable works with credits, process, media metadata, case details and contextual search language.
- Healthcare/finance/legal: higher source/credential accuracy stakes, explicit review and boundaries; don't publish unsupported YMYL advice.
- International: launch only truly supported markets/languages, with translated **value**, correct legal/commercial availability and canonical/hreflang behavior.

## 6. What to deliver

For new/rebuilt public commercial sites: working discovery-ready pages and relevant content, documented current coverage, actionable open gaps, actual testing evidence, a *bounded prioritized growth backlog* and specific monitoring plan. For patch: only affected SEO fixes and verification. For audit: findings/hypotheses with inspected source/state and proposed fixes; do not claim production changes. Don't publish, buy outreach, change sitewide indexing settings, create paid platform accounts or generate mass pages without authority.
