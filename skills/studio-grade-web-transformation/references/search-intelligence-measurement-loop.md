# Search intelligence, competitor research and measured growth

**Use:** real public growth work, audits and postlaunch optimization. **Do not** attach a false business metric to a static HTML lint pass. Links and schema alone cannot verify rankings, citations, search demand or a win over competitors.

## Evidence ledger and accounts

| Source (only if accessed) | Can legitimately observe | Cannot infer |
|---|---|---|
| Google Search Console | Search pages, queries, impressions, clicks, country/device, index/URL inspection, separate 2026 generative AI impression report | Competitors' private traffic, why algorithms ranked a URL |
| Bing Webmaster Tools | Search/crawl, AI Performance citations/grounding phrases and possibly Topics Portfolio beta | All ChatGPT citations, guaranteed AI inclusion or customer conversion |
| First-party consented analytics | Actual referral/campaign, events, quality, activation/revenue by eligible cohort | Complete source attribution if AI/referrer is missing |
| Live SERP observation | Snapshot of actual results/features for a given location, device, date, query | Permanent or population-wide rank and market share |
| Site/server/CDN logs | Access from identified crawler UA/IP (verify documentation/IP), live HTTP status, redirects, response failures | Search eligibility solely because a bot requested a URL |
| Keyword/market tools | Third-party volume/competition estimates with source/date/method | Certain demand/market share |
| User/customer/support evidence | Real language, obstacles, completion and satisfaction | Search-engine causality unless paired with search data |

Never silently ask for credentials, collect personal data, connect apps, or publish changes to Search Console, robots, Bing or merchant listings without permission. If relevant accounts/tools are absent, continue from **public evidence and code** with `UNKNOWN` statuses.

## Baseline and competitor opportunity sheet

For each **valuable intent cluster**, record: query family, market/language/device, audience/funnel job, verified observed SERP URL/type (or unknown), current URL, query/landing metrics and date/source, competitor proof gaps, product-specific reason to deserve attention, next action, priority, and follow-up date. A competitor's proprietary conversion rate, backlink quality and traffic are **not** facts unless independently sourced.

Separate **business competitors** (substitute products) from **SERP competitors** (sites actually appearing on queries) and **AI answer sources** (cited pages). Build a source-date snapshot; use consistent geography/device when comparing. If the market is unresearched, do not claim "outranks 100% of competitors".

## Diagnostic priority flow (rather than magical 100-point score)

1. **No eligible traffic:** inspect HTTP/robots/noindex/canonical/availability/index coverage and whether target pages serve real user intent.
2. **Indexed but underexposed:** check actual demand, intent fit, originality/proof, content gaps, credible mentions and internal link discovery.
3. **Impressions but few qualified visits:** analyze query-to-title/snippet/result-type match and expected visitor outcome; Google can rewrite snippets. Don't presume low CTR means just bad headline.
4. **Visits without useful completion:** validate speed/UX/accessibility, offer/proof/terms, CTA and full downstream flow; content cannot outrank a broken signup.
5. **AI presence unknown/weak:** check platform controls and crawler eligibility, authoritative public content, Google generative AI visibility, Bing citation evidence; no AI-only keyword magic.
6. **Visible but declining:** compare dated actual queries, competitor change, index/crawl regressions, content freshness, seasonality and attribution; do not blame one update without evidence.

Prioritize P0 blockers (inadvertent noindex, wrong canonical, 404 critical URL, deceptive information, inaccessible conversion), then valuable opportunities with a genuine primary evidence advantage. Cost, urgency, owner permission and reversibility matter; no fictional "impact=9.8".

## Metric definitions that avoid vanity

- **Eligible nonbrand organic sessions**: define hostnames, referral classifications, exclusions and bot filtering; reported from owned analytics when consent/availability permits.
- **Qualified organic outcome rate**: legitimate activated trials, qualified inquiries or fulfilled purchases divided by specified eligible organic cohort/time window.
- **Search visibility**: clicks/impressions by canonical URL, query, locale/device and period, not "SEO score".
- **Competitive presence:** sample-based visibility across a *predeclared query basket* with date, market, device and SERP types. Call this `observed sample visibility`, not absolute market share.
- **AI answer participation:** Google generative AI *impressions*, Bing AI Performance *citation events/pages/grounding queries*, plus actual qualified referral downstream. These are not interchangeable units.
- **Authority:** editorially earned relevant references/mentions with evidence of existence, context and quality; avoid raw link-count goals.
- **Risk and health:** crawl/index errors, duplicate/canonical conflicts, structured-data warnings, disapproved feeds, accessibility, page responsiveness and 404 regressions.

When reporting change, supply baseline, post period, count/denominator, source and possible confounds. Search changes are observational unless the experiment design supports causality. Improving Core Web Vitals does not prove organic ranking lift.

## Monitoring cadence chosen by evidence

- On release/migration: verify critical URLs, robots, redirects, canonical, sitemap, rendered content, conversion and tracking; watch immediate search errors as available.
- With enough data: review issue trend and commercial funnel, search terms and pages by intent, credible competitor changes, content freshness and AI reports; prioritize next high-impact action.
- Recurrence must be scheduled by actual owner workflow and data availability; don't claim an agent monitors in the background absent automation.
- Reassess topic/user needs when new product features, region, competitors or platform policies materially change.

## Mandatory honesty and safety

- No paid link schemes, fabricated authority, fake competitor analytics, repeated search queries used as manipulated "rank wins", synthetic user testimonials or spamming platforms.
- Do not optimize pages simply to lure a query unrelated to the actual offer.
- No guarantee to rank #1, beat all sites in all niches, force AI citations or gain instant indexed coverage.
- Don't confuse search and ad targeting, or app-store optimization with ordinary web SEO.
- Preserve deliberate opt-outs: robots.txt and Google's 2026 generative AI control may reflect explicit policy and must not be flipped automatically.
- Rankings, indexing, conversions, competitors and citations remain `UNKNOWN` without actual observational data.
