# Copy lab and ethical psychology — write a reason to believe

**Load for:** public product marketing, offer, homepage hero, pricing, signup, service inquiry or major copy rewrite. Read `positioning-and-copywriting.md` first when market category or differentiation is unclear. Examples below are fictional illustrations; **no performance metrics or product claims are being asserted**.

## 1. Write the message map, not filler

For each consequential claim record: `audience situation → concrete problem → useful outcome → actual mechanism → inspectable proof → objection → literal next action`. If proof is unavailable, phrase a truthful demonstration or omit the claim. Convert features using "This lets [audience] do [job] when [circumstance], because [observable mechanism]" — not "unlock your potential."

Draft three **substantively different** opening candidates when useful:
- **Direct:** category + job + concrete outcome (fastest comprehension).
- **Situational:** a lived problem/context + a tangible relief (immediate relevance).
- **Authorial:** memorable brand expression **paired with** a clear explanatory subtitle and real demonstration (when a creative identity matters).

Judge variants *in the actual layout next to real proof*, not as isolated LLM prose. An expressive hero may be best for a strong brand. No formula, word-count limit, power-word list or emoji quota is universal.

## 2. Seven editing passes with an actual deletion decision

1. **Truth:** attribute factual comparisons, customer names, numerical results, pricing, trial, stock, deadlines, risk, AI capabilities and guarantees. `UNKNOWN` does not become a launch claim.
2. **Instant legibility:** what it is, whom it serves and why care can be inferred from headline + context + real visual. Avoid forcing the user to decode an inspirational slogan.
3. **Specificity:** replace empty adjectives with audience verbs, inputs, outputs, constraint, example or factual demonstration.
4. **Information scent:** headings answer distinct user questions; CTA labels describe the actual next screen/action.
5. **Human rhythm:** preserve voice, idiom, humor and sentence variety. Do not manufacture artificial "human mistakes".
6. **Objection proximity:** put the answer where uncertainty occurs — before a form, near pricing, next to an integration/permission step.
7. **CUT/MERGE:** delete repeated meaning, ornamental slogans and text that the visual already proves; add detail only when decision stakes require it.

### Worked opening examples — conceptual, not product claims

| Use | Weak | Better, when evidence supports it | Proof scene | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B2B CRM | "Revolutionize your business with AI" | "See which leads need a reply before your team opens another spreadsheet." | Actual queue sorted from real declared signals, visible source and next step | "See a working example" |
| Conversational education | "Unlock a world of fluency" | "Practise ordering coffee in English — speak, get feedback, and try again." | Real voice interaction + transcript + retry, or clearly labeled concept | "Try a practice conversation" |
| Video editor portfolio | "Elevating stories to new heights" | "Watch the cut. Then see how it was built." | Actual licensed before/after edit, context and credits | "View the project" |

The examples are **directions**, not licenses to assert that a real product has those features. Check code and offer before reuse.

## 3. Buyer objection table

| Buyer question | Evidence to reveal | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Does it actually work? | A real completed user task, case or honest limitation | Fake interface, fabricated demo and motion as proof |
| Is it for me? | Relevant situation, eligibility and examples | "For everyone" and invented personas |
| Why this, not the alternative? | Specific mechanism, cost or workflow difference | Unsubstantiated "the best" |
| Can I trust it? | Dated attribution, authentic customer statement, privacy/terms | Unverified logos, fake reviews and security badges |
| What do I pay/commit? | Actual price, renewal/cancellation, constraints, total cost | Dark-pattern copy and concealment |
| What happens after click? | Accurate CTA label + small relevant reassurance | "Get started" leading to unexpected payment |
| What if I fail? | Recoverable validation error and next step | "Something went wrong" without recourse |

### Microcopy must be tied to behavior
- `Enviar` → `Solicitar orçamento` *only if a real request is sent*.
- `Começar agora` → `Experimentar uma conversa` *only if the user reaches a working practice*.
- `Erro` → `Não foi possível enviar. Confira a conexão e tente novamente; seus dados continuam aqui.` *only if form state is truly preserved*.
- `Sem cartão` is forbidden unless the actual signup flow needs no card.
- Use persistent form labels, appropriate autofill/type, inline recovery and explicit success/failure feedback. Place concise privacy explanation where data is requested.

## 4. Psychology is a mechanism to assess, not a bag of tricks

| Principle | Legitimate design decision | Misuse / warning |
|---|---|---|
| Cognitive fluency / hierarchy | One clear priority, good grouping, scannable informative headings | Ultra-minimal emptiness that hides critical facts |
| Social proof | Credible, relevant, attributable cases/reviews with denominators | Invented popularity, synthetic quotes or implied endorsement |
| Uncertainty reduction | Demonstration, terms, reliable support and useful error states | Manipulative reassurance or unearned "100% safe" |
| Default effects | Reversible sensible default clearly shown | Prechecked paid upsell / consent |
| Anchoring | Honest comparisons of actual plans/products | Fictional "previous" price to imply a discount |
| Scarcity | True stock/deadline and clearly stated terms | Resetting timers / endless "only two left" |
| Commitment | User-controlled steps proportional to the benefit | Forced sign-up before showing basic information |
| Emotional narrative | A distinct story built from real product and brand signals | Empty emotional promises disconnected from product |

Important: the presence of a research-backed *bias* does **not** prove that exploiting it will increase this product's conversion, or justify deception. Persuasion should help the visitor make a better informed, voluntary decision. See [scarcity meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jretai.2022.06.003), [default effects research](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21707203/), [popularity bias in reviews](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33734776/).

## 5. Anti-AI-slop text lint — editorial, not a literal banned-word scanner

Challenge language such as: "revolutionize", "unleash", "supercharge", "seamless", "next-generation", "transform your journey", "empower your business", "elevate", "unlock", "cutting-edge", and PT-BR "potencialize", "solução inovadora", "eleve", "jornada transformadora", "o futuro chegou". These are **suspect when empty**, not prohibited words. Keep a distinctive brand line when it is coherent and supported by concrete nearby context.

Delete or rewrite:
- Claims transferable unchanged to five unrelated products.
- Headings that are the same benefit in different adjectives.
- Six symmetrical cards created only to fill screen space.
- Fake case studies, reviews, star scores, price anchoring or urgency.
- "No credit card", privacy guarantees or instant results unsupported by the actual flow.
- FAQ questions invented because a page template needs a FAQ.
- SEO keyword repetition and empty pseudo-authority.

**Critical reviewer prompt:** "Read the actual screen without the author's explanation. Quote the weakest claims; link each to the source or lack of source; mark confidence; propose a shorter, stronger copy + more suitable visual proof; list at least one section to cut/merge; verify CTA destination and user commitment. Do not invent customers, conversions, studies or legal assurances."

## 6. Proof requirements before committing public copy

For each business-critical sentence record `text | placement | exact evidence source | verification status | risks | next action`. `VERIFIED` means observed/checked, not merely repeated by a model. If a claim was supplied but unverified, seek authoritative internal approval or leave it labeled/removed. Design presentation does not launder a false claim into truth.
