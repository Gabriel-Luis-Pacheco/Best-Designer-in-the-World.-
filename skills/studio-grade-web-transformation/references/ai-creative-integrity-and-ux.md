# AI-assisted craft, anti-AI slop, and UX of AI products

Two separate tasks: **using AI to design/build** and **designing a product that uses AI**. Neither calls for a default purple gradient, sparkle, three cards, chat UI or fake "AI engine".

## Reliable generative workflow
Feed context: concrete ICP/problem, verified product behavior, existing brand canon and originals, real objections, claim/evidence register, relevant visual precedents **with reasons**, constraints, rights and no-go patterns. If absent, be honest about hypotheses. Use generation for alternatives, prototypes, copy critique, edge cases, tokens and tests; don't outsource final factual, legal or accessibility judgement.

When creative direction is unsettled, ask for 2–5 DIVERGENT concepts, each with thesis, proof scene, spatial/material/behavioral signature, mobile form, motion purpose, feasibility and distinct failure risk. Change the input evidence if options are all interchangeable. Never claim an idea is original because the model says so.

## Recognize and repair slop
- Headline "revolutionize/unlock/empower" → customer situation + mechanism + believable outcome.
- Fake dashboards, users, metrics, stars, quotes, logos → actual functionality/provenance or clearly labeled concept.
- Unrelated stock-style people, synthetic customer portraits → licensed authored assets with honest context.
- Multiple repeated benefit cards, emoji as primary product UI, text disguised as cards → design the actual interaction or useful evidence medium.
- Generic glow/glass, excessive borders, giant titles, negative space with no function → purposeful visual grammar and hierarchy.
- Repainted/reinvented mascots → canonical artwork with fidelity check.
- Motion/3D "because premium" → causal interaction and user benefit or remove.

**Logo swap test:** if the page still fits five competitors, add ownable product-specific substance, not decorative complexity. Preserve human approvals in a lightweight record; do not fabricate interviews or independent reviews.

## Provenance, license and responsibility
Check rights to images, type, music, assets and names. Generative provenance claims are not authenticity proofs; [C2PA](https://c2pa.org/) may record supported origin/edit metadata, not content truth. Verify sensitive claims manually; preserve source trails and identify what was not reviewed. [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) supports a risk-based lens.

## UX of probabilistic features
Identify what AI can/cannot do; expose waiting/streaming, partial/empty, retry/cancel, editing/rejection, source inspection when available, uncertainty without invented percentage, errors/refusal and user recovery. Use appropriate native UX (diff, preview, inline suggestion, approval checklist, map, timeline, structured comparison) rather than forced chatbot. Avoid auto-actions with consequential side effects without review; account for privacy, bias, accessibility, latency and fallback.
